# Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Objective
Identify what's driving low profitability despite strong sales, 
using EDA in Plotly.

## Key Findings
- Discounts above 20% push profit into net losses (r = -0.22)
- Furniture generates 742K in sales but only 18K profit (2.5% margin)
- Central region (Illinois & Texas) applies ~30% avg discount — nearly 
  double other regions — driving most of the losses

## Recommendation
Cap discounts at 20% overall, 10% max on Furniture

## Tools
Plotly (Express & Graph Objects), Pandas, Python
